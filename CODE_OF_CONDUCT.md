# Code of Conduct

Everyone participating in SovraiOS issues, pull requests, reviews, and other
project spaces is expected to help keep them welcoming and useful.

- Be respectful of different backgrounds, experience levels, and viewpoints.
- Give constructive feedback about the work, and accept feedback in good faith.
- Avoid harassment, threats, discriminatory language, personal attacks, and
  unwanted sexual attention.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish another person's private information without
  permission, or share credentials or private conversations.
- Keep discussions relevant and avoid spam or repeated disruption.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report concerns privately to a project maintainer listed in
[CODEOWNERS](.github/CODEOWNERS), using a private contact method on their GitHub
profile. If none is listed, request a private contact in an issue without naming
affected people or sharing incident details. If a maintainer is involved, contact
another listed maintainer.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close or lock discussions, and
restrict participation to protect the community. They should consider context
and severity, explain decisions when appropriate, and share reports only with
people needed to resolve them. These expectations also apply to maintainers.
